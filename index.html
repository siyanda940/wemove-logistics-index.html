We move Logistics

We Move Logistics is a proudly South African logistics and removals company based in Ekurhuleni and Midrand, Gauteng. We are dedicated to providing reliable, affordable, and professional moving and transportation solutions.

We specialize in residential and commercial relocations, furniture removals, packing, storage, and logistics services. Whether you're relocating your home, moving your office, transporting valuable furniture, or requiring secure storage, our experienced team ensures your belongings are handled with the utmost care from collection to delivery.

  • Proof of ID required for high-value item collection.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are your delivery times? +
Local Gauteng deliveries are often same-day or next-day. Long distance (Durban/Cape Town) takes 1-3 days depending on the service type selected.
Do you offer insurance for goods? +
Yes, we offer basic liability coverage. For high-value items, we recommend purchasing additional transit insurance which we can arrange.
How do I track my shipment? +
Please contact our support team via WhatsApp or phone with your order reference number, and we will provide you with real-time updates on your delivery.
Do you do international deliveries? +
We specialize in South African logistics but can arrange cross-border transport to neighboring countries (Namibia, Botswana, Zimbabwe, etc.) upon request.
